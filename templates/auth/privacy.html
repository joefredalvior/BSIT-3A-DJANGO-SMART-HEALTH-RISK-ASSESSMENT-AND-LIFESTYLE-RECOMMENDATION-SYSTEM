What we collect. Account details (name, username, email), optional profile details (birthdate, sex, height, activity level, family history, existing conditions), and the health measurements and lifestyle answers you submit in assessments.

Why we collect it. To calculate your risk score, generate personalised recommendations, track changes over time, prefill future assessments, and send optional report or reminder emails.

Who can see it. Your assessments are private to your account. Authorised clinicians and administrators can view assessment reports for care and system administration. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it. We keep your data until you delete your account or ask us to remove it. Deleting an assessment removes it immediately; deleting your account removes your profile, assessments, goals, and related records.

Your controls. You can export a machine-readable copy of your data, delete individual assessments, permanently delete your account, and opt out of reminder emails from your profile.

Security. We use password hashing, CSRF protection, login lockouts, email verification, and access controls. No online system is perfectly secure, so use a unique password and keep your email account protected.

Medical disclaimer. HealthWise is an educational screening aid, not a diagnosis or a substitute for professional medical advice. Seek care from a qualified clinician for medical concerns.
